# card_pin recommended decisions (advisory only)

These recommendations were generated by an LLM evaluator during calibration
preparation. They are **not** human approvals and must not be copied verbatim
into `v1/card_pin_inclusion_review.csv` until a designated human reviewer
confirms or rejects them. `reviewer_id` and `reviewed_at` in that CSV remain
blank until the real review is recorded.

## Case 1 — banking77-test-0553

- Source category: `pin_blocked`
- Source input: "How do I reset my PIN?"
- Recommended decision: `exclude`
- Recommended primary intent: `pin_reset`
- Recommended rationale: The primary intent is resetting an existing PIN,
  not obtaining or setting up a card PIN. Under the documented
  `Human-reviewed inclusion criteria` in
  [`v1/card_pin_coverage_review.md`](v1/card_pin_coverage_review.md), PIN
  reset is an exclusion reason.

## Case 2 — banking77-test-0524

- Source category: `pin_blocked`
- Source input: "I accidentally blocked my PIN. How do I reset it?"
- Recommended decision: `exclude`
- Recommended primary intent: `pin_reset`
- Recommended rationale: The primary intent is resetting or unblocking an
  accidentally blocked PIN, not obtaining or setting up a card PIN. This is
  also excluded by the documented criteria.

## Required human action

A designated human reviewer must independently confirm or reject each
recommended decision, independent of these recommendations. The confirmed
decisions (the real reviewer's own wording) must then be written into
`v1/card_pin_inclusion_review.csv` by the reviewer, along with their genuine
`reviewer_id` and `reviewed_at`.

After the CSV is complete, the approved manifest can be validated against the
runner's `ensure_manifest_approved()` gate.
